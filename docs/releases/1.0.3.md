GamePack 1.0.3 makes the transition from playback to drawing more immediate.

- Selecting Pen, Arrow, or Ellipse pauses on the current native video frame, without seeking backward to an older playback sample.
- Changing drawing tools keeps the paused frame in place. The timeline settles as soon as the pause is confirmed.
- The compact Moment/Play control sits below the pointer, with distinct icons and tooltips instead of a persistent text label.
- Settings → Moments identifies both drawing modes and offers Default Play speed for timed drawing.

Requires macOS 14 or later, or Windows 10 version 2004 or later. Libraries and existing reviews remain compatible with GamePack 1.0.2.
