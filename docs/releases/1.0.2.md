GamePack 1.0.2 smooths out the review controls and makes moment markers clearer.

- Checking for updates keeps the footer and action button in place as the status changes.
- Switching between Moment and Play keeps the drawing tools steady. Click Play again to end timed capture; unfinished drawings remain available through Resume.
- Tool hints dismiss when a tool is activated.
- Space plays or pauses after drawing a moment. Clicking the comment field or Reply still enters text editing.
- Replay and Reply have more room inside comment cards. Posting keeps the card, video frame, and playhead in place.
- Seek anywhere on the video timeline while creating a timed annotation. Existing strokes retain their original timing.
- Choose 0.25×, 0.5×, 0.75×, 1×, 2×, or 4× playback. Timed annotations start at 0.5×; change their default in Settings → Playback. Regular playback keeps its own speed.
- Nearby moments show their individual timeline positions. A numbered marker counts conversations on the same frame; grouped dots indicate nearby timestamps.

Requires macOS 14 or later, or Windows 10 version 2004 or later. Choose the ARM64 or x64 package for your device and follow the included installation instructions. Windows packages require Developer Mode.

Libraries remain in `GAMEPACK_HOME` or the default `.gamepack` folder. Existing reviews and their file formats remain unchanged. The annotation speed preference is stored locally and remains compatible with older clients.
